# 01 — What real satellite/drone damage-detection data sources can Paramify use?

Type: research
Status: open
Blocked by: (none)

## Question

Which real, accessible data sources can confirm structural damage to a home from bombing/war (satellite imagery, drone feeds, open conflict-damage datasets — e.g. UNOSAT, Sentinel-1/2, Planet, commercial APIs)? For each: cost, coverage (which regions), latency (how fast after an event), and whether damage confirmation is human-verified or algorithmic. The answer decides whether the production oracle is real-data-backed and what the demo simulation should mimic.
