# 02 — How does the owner lookup map a property to a wallet?

Type: research
Status: open
Blocked by: (none)

## Question

Which government property registry (UK Land Registry, US county assessor, etc.) can identify the owner of a geolocated home, and how does that identity map to a blockchain wallet for payout? What are the API costs, access requirements, and demo alternatives (a mock gov DB)? The answer defines the demo's gov-database layer and the production onboarding flow (how a homeowner links their wallet to their registered property).
